WHAT HELPS US FIX IT

  • Your device model and iOS or Android version
  • The MotoCoach app version shown on your device
  • Which feature was affected: lap timer, GPS, video, sessions or garage
  • What you expected and what actually happened
  • A screenshot or brief screen recording, if safe to share
