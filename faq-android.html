01 / Android 2.0 preview

Can I download MotoCoach 2.0 from Google Play today?+

Not yet. Android 2.0 is being prepared for Google Play closed testing and is not a public Play Store download. Contact support if you are interested in testing when available.

What is included in the Android preview?+

The development version includes a redesigned track dashboard, manual pit-wall lap timer, local GPS session tracking and Video + GPS workflows, lap-time summaries and a garage notebook.

Is the Android AI coach online?+

No. The current 2.0 analysis is grounded in saved lap times and uses sample-labelled demo data where appropriate. Connected AI coaching and paid subscriptions are future work, not live features.

02 / Device requirements and data

Does the Android app need an account?+

The current Android development version does not require an account or login.

Why does the app need location, camera and microphone permissions?+

Location supports GPS speed and lap timing. Camera supports optional Video + GPS recording. Microphone may be used when you choose to capture audio with video. You can decline permissions; some capture features will not work without them.

Where does my GPS recording go?+

Session and telemetry data are saved on your device. Sharing a file is user-initiated, using the app or service you select. Read our Android privacy policy for details.

Is Android 2.0 official timing?+

No. It is a training aid and must not replace official race timing, a transponder or track safety equipment.

03 / Trackday use

Can I use the manual lap timer without GPS?+

Yes. The separate manual timer is intended for a helper on the pit wall to time completed laps, regardless of the rider's GPS recording.

Does the app work on motorcycles, cars and karts?+

The 2.0 preview includes vehicle setup options for bikes, cars and karts. Camera or GPS reliability varies by device and mounting arrangement.

Can I operate the phone while driving or riding?+

No. Prepare and secure the device before moving. Always focus on the track and follow your organiser's safety instructions.

SAFETY FIRST. MotoCoach is for closed-circuit training. Never interact with your phone while moving and never treat phone GPS as official race timing.
