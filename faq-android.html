MotoCoach – FAQ

Android version

General

What is MotoCoach?

MotoCoach is a track-day lap timing and session recording app designed for closed-circuit use. It provides lap timing and optional Video + GPS recording for training and testing.

Is MotoCoach for road use?

No. MotoCoach is designed for use on closed circuits such as track days and test sessions. It should not be used while riding or driving on public roads.

Video + GPS

How does Video + GPS work?

You set a start line on the circuit, arm the session, and once you pass the start speed, MotoCoach records video and detects laps automatically using GPS.

Does Video + GPS need mobile signal?

No. Once started, lap timing and video recording work offline. A data connection is only required for app updates or opening support links.

Data & Privacy

Do I need an account?

No. MotoCoach does not require accounts or logins. All session data is stored locally on your device.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. MotoCoach does not upload session data to external servers. Your data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up through your system.

Safety

Is MotoCoach an official timing system?

No. MotoCoach is for personal training use only and is not an official race timing system.

Can MotoCoach control my vehicle?

No. MotoCoach does not control your motorcycle, car, or kart. You are always responsible for how and where you ride or drive.
