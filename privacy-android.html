Privacy Policy

Last updated: 04 January 2026

About MotoCoach

MotoCoach is a mobile app for closed-circuit track day lap timing and session recording features. This Privacy Policy explains what information is used by the app and how it is handled.

Information you provide

  • Session details: track name, rider/bike names, notes, and lap times that you type into the app.

This information is stored locally on your device.

Device permissions

MotoCoach may request the following permissions to provide its core features:

  • Location: used to measure speed and detect lap timing (start/finish line and lap triggers).
  • Camera: used to record video during a session if you use the Video + GPS feature.
  • Microphone (optional): used only if you enable audio recording with video.

Data sharing

MotoCoach does not sell your personal information. The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Analytics & diagnostics

The app may rely on standard platform diagnostics (for example crash reports) provided by your device or app store. These reports are typically anonymous and used to improve stability.

Data retention

Session data is stored on your device and can be removed by deleting the app. If your device includes system backups, your backup settings may affect whether app data is included in backups.

Children

MotoCoach is intended for adult riders and is not designed for children under 16 years of age.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email motocoachapp@gmail.com.
