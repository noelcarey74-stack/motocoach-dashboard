01 / General

What is MotoCoach?+

MotoCoach is a track-day companion for motorcyclists, car drivers, kart racers and pit crews. It combines tools for recording laps and sessions with lap history, vehicle setup notes and a redesigned 2.0 interface in development.

Can mechanics or friends time laps from the pit wall?+

Yes. The 2.0 development version has a separate manual lap timer with large start and lap controls. A crew member can time laps from the side of the circuit without riding or driving with the device.

Is the new Android 2.0 version available yet?+

Not yet. Android 2.0 is being prepared for closed testing on Google Play. The site video and app screenshots show the development preview. The iPhone version is already available on the App Store, although features differ.

Do I need a separate MotoCoach account?+

The Android 2.0 development app does not require an account. Local sessions are saved on the device. The available iPhone product manages purchases through Apple where applicable.

02 / Video and GPS

Will GPS lap timing work without a mobile data signal?+

Supported phone GPS can operate without mobile data, subject to the device receiving suitable satellite signals. App downloads, purchases and online features can require a connection. Timing accuracy depends on the phone and conditions.

Is MotoCoach accurate enough for official racing?+

No. MotoCoach is a training and track-day tool, not certified race timing. GPS speed and lap timing are affected by hardware, signal quality, sampling rate and how the start line was set.

Can I watch the recording with telemetry afterwards?+

The Android 2.0 development build includes a Video + GPS recording and review workflow on supported devices. Captured readings are limited to data the phone actually records; lean angle and braking pressure are not automatically available.

Will mounting my phone damage it?+

Strong motorcycle vibration can affect some phone cameras. Use a suitable vibration-dampened, track-organiser-approved mounting solution. Secure the device before moving and protect it from weather and debris.

03 / Data, payments and safety

Is the 2.0 race intelligence generated by AI?+

The current Android 2.0 development preview uses real saved lap-time calculations and clearly labelled demonstration examples. Advanced connected AI coaching is still in development, so the app does not claim to measure lean angle, tyre grip or braking force without sensors.

Are there Android subscription payments?+

No. Android 2.0 is not publicly available yet, and its Play Billing area is a development preview. No Google Play purchase flow has been launched. iPhone subscriptions, where offered, are managed through Apple's App Store.

Where are my sessions and recordings stored?+

The current Android development build saves lap, telemetry and recording information locally on the phone, with user-selected export or sharing options. For full details, read the Android privacy policy.

Can I interact with the app on track?+

Never operate a phone while riding or driving. Set up any recording while stationary before leaving the paddock. Follow circuit regulations and track-day briefings, and use a pit-wall helper for manual lap timing.

SAFETY FIRST. MotoCoach is for closed-circuit training. Never interact with your phone while moving and never treat phone GPS as official race timing.
