More frequently asked questions ↗
Can a mechanic use MotoCoach on the pit wall?+

Yes. The 2.0 development build includes a separate manual stopwatch and session fields designed for pit-wall helpers. It does not require them to be on the motorcycle or in the car.

Does it work for bikes, cars and karts?+

The MotoCoach 2.0 design supports a vehicle choice for bikes, cars and karts. The feature set and availability can differ between the currently available iPhone app and the unreleased Android version.

Is the Android app on Google Play yet?+

No. MotoCoach 2.0 for Android is being prepared for Google Play testing. The screenshots and video on this page demonstrate that development version, not a live Android store download.

Is the race analysis real AI telemetry?+

The 2.0 preview uses recorded lap times to calculate useful summaries; example charts are explicitly marked sample data. It cannot infer lean angle, grip or exact braking points without appropriate sensors. More advanced AI coaching is planned.

Can I operate the app while riding or driving?+

No. Mount and configure devices safely while stationary. Never interact with an app while moving, and follow circuit rules and official track-day briefings.

Do I need an expensive data logger?+

The phone-based features are designed to work without a separate dedicated timing system. Device GPS and camera quality affect results; a phone does not replace an official timing transponder.
